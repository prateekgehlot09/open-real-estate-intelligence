# About Titan Crest

Titan Crest is a real estate investment intelligence firm operating in Dubai.

We use OREIL as the analytical foundation for every client engagement — validating
yield assumptions, scoring investment risk, and generating structured investment
analysis grounded in data rather than projection.

---

## What We Do Differently

Most brokerages present opportunities. Titan Crest validates them.

Before any asset recommendation reaches a client, it passes through the same
yield validation, risk scoring, and projection gap analysis that powers this
open-source framework.
